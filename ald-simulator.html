Physics behind the simulation

d = N × GPC + Δd
T ≈ exp(−2κd)
C = kε₀A / d (d in meters)
EOT = d × 3.9 / k
t = N × 5 s

Assumptions: HfO₂ relative permittivity k = 20; κ = 3 nm⁻¹; area = 1 µm²; ε₀ = 8.8541878128 × 10⁻¹² F/m; cycle time = 5 s.

Educational parametric model inspired by advanced transistor scaling. Assumes continuous, uniform films and a simplified rectangular tunneling barrier. T is a dimensionless transmission factor, not measured leakage current. The model does not reproduce IBM's proprietary technology or validate sub-1 nm film fabrication. Technology-node names are not physical film thicknesses.
